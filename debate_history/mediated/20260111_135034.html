Let σ ∈ S10. Its cycle decomposition yields disjoint cycle lengths that form a partition of 10; the order of σ is the least common multiple (lcm) of those cycle lengths. We must choose positive integers summing to 10 so as to maximize their lcm.

Consider the largest part m in the partition.

- If m ≥ 9: then m = 9 or 10. If m = 10 the lcm = 10 ≤ 30. If m = 9 the remaining part sum is 1, so lcm ≤ 9 < 30.
- If m = 8: the remaining sum is 2, so the partition is 8+2 or 8+1+1; in either case the lcm ≤ lcm(8,2)=8·1=8 < 30.
- If m = 7: the remaining sum is 3, so possible partitions are 7+3, 7+2+1, 7+1+1+1. In all cases the lcm ≤ 7·3 = 21 < 30.
- If m ≤ 6: then all parts are ≤6. Check partitions with largest lcm among these:
  - 6+4 (sum 10) gives lcm(6,4)=12.
  - 6+3+1 gives lcm = 6.
  - 5+5 gives lcm = 5.
  - 5+4+1 gives lcm(5,4)=20.
  - 5+3+2 gives lcm(5,3,2)=30.
  - Any other partition with parts ≤6 yields lcm ≤ 30 and is not larger than 30.

From these cases, no partition of 10 yields lcm strictly greater than 30, and the partition 5+3+2 attains lcm = 30.
