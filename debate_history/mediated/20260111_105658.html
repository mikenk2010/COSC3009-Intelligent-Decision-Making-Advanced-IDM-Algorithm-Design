📝 Question

Janet's ducks lay eggs. She gets 3 times as many eggs from her ducks as she gets from her chickens. If she gets 3 eggs from her chickens, how many eggs does she get from her ducks?

Round 0
🤖 Agent A - Initial Answer
Domain  

Arithmetic / elementary algebra

Clarification / Key Reasoning  

Let the number of eggs from chickens be \(C\) and from ducks be \(D\). The problem states \(D\) is 3 times \(C\), i.e.  

\[

D = 3C.

\]

Given \(C = 3\), substitute to get

\[

D = 3\cdot 3 = 9.

\]

Final Answer  

Janet gets \(\boxed{9}\) eggs from her ducks.
