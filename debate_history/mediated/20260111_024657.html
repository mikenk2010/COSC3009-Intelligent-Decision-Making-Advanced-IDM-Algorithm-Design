### Problem Restatement

Let \( K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{18}) \) be a field extension of \( \mathbb{Q} \). We need to determine the degree, denoted as [K : Q], of this extension.

### Definitions and Known Results

- **Field Extensions**: Given fields \( E \subseteq F \), the degree of the extension \( [F : E] \) is the dimension over \( E \) of the vector space \( F \).
- **Degree Calculation for Multiple Roots**: If an element in a field extension has multiple roots, we can use these roots to simplify calculations.
- **Lifting Polynomials**: Given two polynomials \( f(x) = (x - z_1)(x - z_2)\dots(x - z_n) \) and \( g(y) = y^n + c_{n-1}y^{n-1}\dots + c_0 \), where \( z_1, z_2,\ldots,z_n \) are roots of the first polynomial in a field extension over \( Q \), one can find polynomials that lift these to the second field.

### Proof

To determine the degree [K : Q], we need to consider the degrees of the extensions:

1. **Degree of \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) \) over \( \mathbb{Q} \)**:
   - The minimal polynomial for \( \sqrt{2} \) over \( \mathbb{Q} \) is \( x^2 - 2 \), which has degree 2.
   - Therefore, [ \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) : \mathbb{Q} \) ] = 2.

2. **Degree of \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}) \) over \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) \)**:
   - The minimal polynomial for \( \sqrt{3} \) over \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) \) is \( x^2 - 3 \), which has degree 2.
   - Therefore, [ \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}) : \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) \) ] = 2.

3. **Degree of \( K \) over \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}) \)**:
   - Consider the element \( \sqrt[4]{18} \). Note that \( 18 = 2 \cdot 9 \), so we can consider its powers modulo these factors.
     - The minimal polynomial for \( \sqrt[4]{18} \) over \( \mathbb{Q}( \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}) \) is \( x^4 - 18 \), which has degree 4.
   - Therefore, [ \( K : \mathbb{Q}( \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}) \) ] = 4.

Since the first extension is over a second extension that itself includes another field (in this case, \( \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[4]{18}) \)), we can use the fact that lifting polynomials from one field to an intermediate field results in reduced degree by at least 2:

\[ [K : Q] = [K : (\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}))][\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}) : Q] = 4 * 2 = 8. \]

### Final Conclusion

The degree of the field extension \( K \) over \( \mathbb{Q} \) is [K : Q] = 8
