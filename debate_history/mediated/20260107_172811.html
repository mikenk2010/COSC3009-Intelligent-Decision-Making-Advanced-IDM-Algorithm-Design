📝 Question

Janet's ducks lay eggs. She gets 3 times as many eggs from her ducks as she gets from her chickens. If she gets 3 eggs from her chickens, how many eggs does she get from her ducks?

Round 0
🤖 Agent A - Initial Answer
### Step 1: Identify Key Information
- Janet gets **3 eggs** from her chickens.
- She gets **3 times** as many eggs from her ducks as she gets from her chickens.

### Step 2: Set Up the Calculation

Let:
- The number of eggs from chickens = $C = 3$
- The number of eggs from ducks = $D$

According to the problem:
$$D = 3 \times C$$

### Step 3: Substitute Known Values

Now, substitute the value of $C$ into the equation:
$$D = 3 \times 3$$

### Step 4: Calculate the Number of Eggs from Ducks

Now perform the multiplication:
$$D = 9$$

### Conclusion

Janet gets **9 eggs** from her ducks.

$$

### Conclusion
