- We must choose positive integers (cycle lengths) summing to $10$ to maximize their $\operatorname{lcm}$.

### Step 2: Candidate partitions and their orders

  $$\operatorname{lcm}(5,3,2) = 5 \times 3 \times 2 = 30$$
