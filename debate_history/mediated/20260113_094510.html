📝 Question

A coffee shop sells coffee for $2.50 per cup and tea for $1.75 per cup. If a customer buys 4 cups of coffee and 3 cups of tea, how much does the customer pay in total?

Round 0
🤖 Agent A - Initial Answer
Domain  

Arithmetic (elementary arithmetic / linear combination).

Clarification / Key Reasoning  

Total cost = (number of coffees)·(price per coffee) + (number of teas)·(price per tea).  

Compute: \(4\cdot \$2.50 + 3\cdot \$1.75 = \$10.00 + \$5.25 = \$15.25.\)

Minimal Generator Check  

Generators used: the four cups of coffee, the three cups of tea, and the given prices \$2.50 and \$1.75. These are precisely the problem data; no additional objects or assumptions were introduced.

Final Answer  

\(\$15.25\)
🤖 Agent B - Initial Answer
Domain  

Arithmetic (elementary monetary computation).

Clarification / Key Reasoning  

Prices and quantities are given: coffee at \$2.50 per cup, tea at \$1.75 per cup. Total cost is sum of cost of coffees and cost of teas:
\[

\text{Cost}_{\text{coffee}}=4\times 2.50=10.00,
\qquad

\text{Cost}_{\text{tea}}=3\times 1.75=5.25,
\]

\[

\text{Total}=\text{Cost}_{\text{coffee}}+\text{Cost}_{\text{tea}}=10.00+5.25=15.25.

\]

Minimal Generator Check  

Minimal required objects: the two unit prices \$2.50 and \$1.75 and the quantities 4 and 3. No other objects were introduced.

Final Answer  

The customer pays \$15.25 in total.
