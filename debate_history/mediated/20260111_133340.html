Let the cycle lengths be a multiset {c_i} with sum ∑ c_i = 10. The prime factors that can appear in the lcm are primes ≤ 10, but any prime p appearing in the lcm requires a cycle of length divisible by p, so at least p points are used for that cycle. In particular:
- If a factor 11 (or larger) were needed it would require at least 11 points, impossible.
- If 7 divides the lcm then some cycle of length ≥7 is present; the only possibility is a 7-cycle plus remaining 3 points, giving lcm divisible by 7 and at most multiplied by lcm of partition of 3, hence lcm ≤ lcm(7,3)=21.
