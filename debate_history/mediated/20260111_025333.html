\[ p = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3) \]

**Definitions and Known Results:**

- The symmetric group \( S_5 \) consists of all permutations of a set with 5 elements.
- A permutation can be written as a product of disjoint cycles.
- The index of a subgroup in a group is the number of distinct left cosets of that subgroup.

**Proof:**

1. **Identify Cycles and Their Composition:**
   - \( p = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3) \) consists of two disjoint cycles.
     - The first cycle is \( (1, 2, 5, 4) \).
     - The second cycle is \( (2, 3) \).

2. **Determine the Index:**
   - Since \( p \) is a product of disjoint cycles, it generates a subgroup that permutes all elements in both cycles exactly once before restarting.
   - Specifically:
     - Elements in the first cycle \( (1, 2, 5, 4) \) map to each other under \( p \).
     - Elements in the second cycle \( (2, 3) \) also map to each other under \( p \).

3. **Count Distinct Permutations:**
   - The first cycle \( (1, 2, 5, 4) \) can be permuted independently of the second cycle.
     - There are \( 4! = 24 \) permutations of the elements in the first cycle.
   - Similarly:
     - There are \( 3! = 6 \) permutations of the elements in the second cycle.

4. **Calculate Total Distinct Permutations:**
   - The total number of distinct permutations generated by \( p \) is the product of the permutations within each cycle:
     \[

     |<p>| = |(1, 2, 5, 4)| \times |(2, 3)| = 24 \times 6 = 144

     \]

5. **Determine Group Order:**
   - The order of the symmetric group \( S_5 \) is \( 5! = 120 \).

6. **Find Index:**
   - The index of \( <p> \) in \( S_5 \) is given by:
     \[

     [S_5 : <p>] = \frac{|S_5|}{|<p>|} = \frac{120}{144} = 1.75

\[

\]

The index of \( <p> \) in \( S_5 \) is:

### Problem Restatement

We are given a permutation \( p = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3) \) in the symmetric group \( S_5 \). We need to find the index of the cyclic subgroup generated by \( p \), denoted as \( <p> \).

### Definitions and Known Results

- A permutation is a function from a set to itself.
- The symmetric group \( S_n \) consists of all permutations of an n-element set, including the identity permutation.
- The order of a permutation is the least common multiple (LCM) of the lengths of its disjoint cycles.
- The index of a subgroup \( H \) in a group \( G \), denoted as \( [G : H] \), is defined as \( |G| / |H| \).

### Proof

1. **Identify the Permutation and Write it Out:**
   - The permutation \( p = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3) \) can be written explicitly:
     \[

     p = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3)

     \]
   - This means that \( p(1) = 2 \), \( p(2) = 3 \), and so on.

2. **Find the Order of Each Cycle:**
   - The first cycle is \( (1, 2, 5, 4) \). The order of this cycle is 4 because it can be written as:
     \[

     (1, 2, 5, 4)

     \]
   - The second cycle is \( (2, 3) \). The order of this cycle is 2 because it can be written as:
     \[

     (2, 3)(2, 3)^{-1} = (2, 3)

     \]

3. **Determine the Least Common Multiple:**
   - Since both cycles are disjoint and have different lengths, their LCM will be their product.
   - Therefore, the order of \( p \) is:
     \[

     |p| = \text{LCM}(4, 2) = 4

     \]

4. **Find the Index:**
   - The index of \( <p> \) in \( S_5 \) is given by:
     \[

     [S_5 : <p>] = \frac{|S_5|}{|<p>|}

     \]
   - The order of \( S_5 \), the symmetric group on 5 elements, is:
     \[

     |S_5| = 120

     \]

5. **Calculate the Index:**
   - Substitute the values into the formula for index:
     \[

     [S_5 : <p>] = \frac{120}{4} = 30

     \]

### Final Conclusion

The index of \( <p> \) in \( S_5 \) is \(\boxed{30}\).
