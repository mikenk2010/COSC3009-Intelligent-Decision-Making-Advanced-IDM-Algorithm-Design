### Step 2: Calculate the Number of Eggs Produced by Ducks

Since ducks produce three times as many eggs as chickens, we multiply the number of eggs produced by chickens:

\[ \text{Number of eggs from ducks} = 3 \times (\text{Number of eggs from chickens}) \]

Substitute the given value for the number of eggs from chickens:

\[ \text{Number of eggs from ducks} = 3 \times 3 \]

### Step 3: Perform the Calculation

Calculate the total number of eggs produced by Janet's ducks:

\[ \text{Number of eggs from ducks} = 9 \]

### Conclusion

Janet gets **9** eggs from her ducks.
