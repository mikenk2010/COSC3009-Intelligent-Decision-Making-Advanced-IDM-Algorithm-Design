📝 Question

Janet's ducks lay eggs. She gets 3 times as many eggs from her ducks as she gets from her chickens. If she gets 3 eggs from her chickens, how many eggs does she get from her ducks?

Round 0
🤖 Agent A - Initial Answer
### Step 1: Identify Key Information
- Janet gets **3 eggs** from her chickens.
- The number of eggs from her ducks is **3 times** the number of eggs from her chickens.

### Step 2: Calculate Eggs from Ducks

To find the number of eggs Janet gets from her ducks, we can set up the equation:

$$

\text{Eggs from Ducks} = 3 \times \text{Eggs from Chickens}

$$

Substituting the known value:

$$

\text{Eggs from Ducks} = 3 \times 3

$$

### Step 3: Perform the Calculation

Now, we calculate the number of eggs from the ducks:

$$

\text{Eggs from Ducks} = 9

$$

### Conclusion

Janet gets **9 eggs** from her ducks.
