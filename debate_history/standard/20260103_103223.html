📝 Question

Sarah reads 15 pages of a book on Monday, 23 pages on Tuesday, and 18 pages on Wednesday. If the book has 200 pages total, how many pages does Sarah have left to read?

Round 0
🤖 Agent A - Initial Answer
To solve the problem step-by-step, we need to calculate how many pages Sarah has read in total over the three days and then subtract that from the total number of pages in the book. 1. **Calculate the total pages read:** - Pages read on Monday: 15 pages - Pages read on Tuesday: 23 pages - Pages read on Wednesday: 18 pages We will add these amounts together: \[ Total pages read = 15 + 23 + 18 \] Let's perform the addition step-by-step: - First, add Monday and Tuesday's pages: \[ 15 + 23 = 38 \] - Now, add Wednesday's pages to the total from Monday and Tuesday: \[ 38 + 18 = 56 \] So, Sarah has read a total of 56 pages. 2. **Calculate the remaining pages:** - The total number of pages in the book is 200 pages. - To find out how many pages Sarah has left to read, we subtract the total pages she has read from the total pages in the book: \[ Pages left = Total pages - Total pages read \] Substituting in the values we have: \[ Pages left = 200 - 56 \] Now, we perform the subtraction: \[ 200 - 56 = 144 \] 3. **Conclusion:** Sarah has 144 pages left to read. Thus, the final answer is that Sarah has **144 pages left** to read.
