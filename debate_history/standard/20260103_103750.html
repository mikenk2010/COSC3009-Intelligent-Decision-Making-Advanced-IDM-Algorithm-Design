📝 Question

Sarah reads 15 pages of a book on Monday, 23 pages on Tuesday, and 18 pages on Wednesday. If the book has 200 pages total, how many pages does Sarah have left to read?

We subtract the total pages read from the total pages in the book: Remaining pages = Total pages - Total pages read Remaining pages = 200 - 56 Calculating this gives: 200 - 56 = **144 pages** ### Conclusion Sarah has **144 pages** left to read.
